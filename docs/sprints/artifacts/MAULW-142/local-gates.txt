## U-S4-JF-02 (J4.4)

```text
$ uv run pytest tests/unit/test_reminder_dst.py -q
28 passed
```

Cases: the 09:00 wall clock is preserved across both New York transitions (only the UTC
instant moves); a nonexistent hour moves to the gap end on the 2026-03-08 New York gap
(`02:00`/`02:30` -> `03:00 EDT`, `07:00 UTC`), on the 30-minute Lord Howe gap and on the
Santiago midnight gap; valid minutes just before/after the gap are untouched; a repeated
hour arms one occurrence at its first fold (`01:30 EDT`, `05:30 UTC`) and the second fold
is never planned; the canonical `dedupe_key` carries the local target day, not the instant,
so both folds share one identity; re-planning before/after the transition returns the same
instant and key; confirmation exactly at the normalised instant keeps the threshold and one
second later omits it; timed reminders stay absolute instants and derive their local
occurrence day across the fold; A4.3 retires the old `due_version`, a disabled threshold,
and never selects `sent`/`cancelled` rows on a DST target; every DST occurrence matches the
canonical builder with unique keys.
