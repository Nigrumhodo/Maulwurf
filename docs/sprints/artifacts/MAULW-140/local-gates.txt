## Fix1 after the hoplite review (2026-11-01)

Changes: the scheduler cron is disabled by default
(`MAULWURF_NOTIFICATION_SCHEDULER_ENABLED=false`) until J4.1 consumes `sending`; the
election, expiry sweep and due comparison use PostgreSQL's transaction clock (`now()`)
instead of each host's wall clock; migration `0018` adds `notifications.claimed_by_fence`
and the atomic claim writes the elected fence so J4.1 can validate it before sending.

The raw sandbox environment still produces the same two pre-existing bracketed-IPv6
`httpx` failures in unit tests (`test_ingest_sink`/`test_llm`) and 19 instances in the ingest
reception integration file; both disappear with a `NO_PROXY` without bracketed IPv6 entries
and are unrelated to this diff. The new scheduler cases cover a host clock far ahead of the
database for election, expiry and due comparison, the persisted fence across a takeover,
the disabled cron and the disabled service, and the `has_table_privilege` guard for
app/jobs/ingest on both `scheduler_leases` and `notifications`.
