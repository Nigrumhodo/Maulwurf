## 6. Contract review (D3-Audio)

Public source consulted, not a measurement of this `function-id`:

- [Service Specific Terms for NVCF Service](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/agreements/cloud-services/service-specific-terms-for-nvcf-service/)

That page, in its data section, says that NVIDIA collects configuration and
operating system data, load metrics (executions and
GPU time), and error and execution logs for tracing and diagnostics.
It states that those logs can be deleted at the customer's choice and are
associated with the enterprise account. It asks for name, email, or
enablement data to deliver the service. It refers processing to a DPA
that this report does not transcribe.

It does not say, for the `Recognize` of this `function-id`:
