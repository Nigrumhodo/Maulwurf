# G6 extraction score (L3.7)

Offline score of the frozen `g6-v1` dataset. The harness is
[`scripts/eval/g6.py`](../../scripts/eval/g6.py) (implementation in
`apps/api/tools/eval/g6.py`). It does not call a model.

> **Status: replay measured, real provider `NO VERIFICADO`.** The ratios below are an
> authored stand-in for recorded model output. They exercise the validators, the date
> resolver and the evidence dedupe. They are not a G6 approval and not P-S3-JL-07.

## Protocol

Matching unit: activity type + normalized date (`date_status`, `due_date`, `due_at` in
UTC) + evidence (segment, Unicode span, verbatim quote). A citation that fails
`validate_analysis` is not a published proposal. Languages are scored separately on the
frozen `test` partition; a global average is not the gate. Prompts are not tuned against
`test`. The `development` partition (4 activities per language) is not part of the gate.

Thresholds, as integer ratios: precision ≥ 95/100, recall ≥ 85/100, exact date ≥ 95/100
on resolvable gold items that share type and evidence with a published proposal. Every
ambiguous gold item must be published with `date_status=ambiguous` and both dates null.
